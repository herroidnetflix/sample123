About this site

This guide is run by Navvora Telugu, a creator community around Telugu cinema content. We noticed that many creators learn about copyright only after a strike, so we publish simple explanations to help them post with confidence.

Editorial approach

  • We explain laws in plain language and link to official sources.
  • Our pages are general information, not legal advice, and we are not a law firm.
  • Laws and platform rules change, so each page shows a review date.
  • If you spot a mistake, tell us and we will correct it.
