Four myths creators believe

Myth: "No copyright intended" makes it safe.
Fact: That line has no legal effect. Permission from the owner is what counts.
Myth: Giving credit makes it legal.
Fact: Credit is courtesy, not a licence.
Myth: A few seconds of a song is always fine.
Fact: There is no safe number of seconds. Short clips are still claimed.
Myth: It is fine if I don't earn money.
Fact: Copyright applies to free posts too. Monetising only raises the risk.
